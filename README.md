## 🏆 The 4 Pillars of FLL Challenge

1. **Core Values**: *Discovery, Innovation, Impact, Inclusion, Teamwork, Fun* + *Gracious Professionalism®* & *Coopertition®*.
2. **Robot Game**: Building & programming autonomous SPIKE Prime robots for 2.5-minute matches.
3. **Robot Design**: Engineering process, mechanical stability, code modularity, and strategy presentation.
4. **Innovation Project**: Researching a real-world problem aligned with the season theme, developing an innovative solution, and sharing it with experts and the community.

---

## 🔑 Golden Rule of Coaching
> **"The kids do the work!"**
> As a coach, your role is to facilitate, inspire, manage logistics, and ask guiding questions—never to build or program for the team.
