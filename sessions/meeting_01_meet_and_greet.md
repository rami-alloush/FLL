# 🤝 Meeting #1 Lesson Plan: Meet & Greet (Students & Parents)

**Season**: FLL Challenge 2026–2027: **BIOGLOW™**  
**Target Age Group**: 9–12 Years Old (Grades 4–7)  
**Total Time**: 2.5 Hours (150 Minutes)  
**Participants**: Students, Parents/Guardians, Coach & Mentors  

---

## 🎯 Meeting Objectives
1. **Welcome & Community**: Build excitement and make every student and parent feel welcomed.
2. **Program & Video Introduction**: Show official FIRST videos and present the 4 Pillars of FLL Challenge (Core Values, Innovation Project, Robot Design, Robot Game) with a focus on this season's **BIOGLOW™** theme.
3. **Icebreaker Engagement**: Facilitate interactive LEGO building challenges for students and parents.
4. **Set Expectations & Logistics**: Align on the **once-a-week, 2.5-hour meeting schedule**, parent support roles, attendance, and the golden rule: *"Kids do the work!"*
5. **Hands-On SPIKE Prime & Video Demos**: Give students their first hands-on touchpoint with the SPIKE Prime hub, motors, and sensors alongside tutorial clips from Prime Lessons.

### Segment 1: Welcome & Sign-In (15 Minutes)
- **Coach Action**: Greet families at the door. Have students create a custom LEGO-brick-themed name badge.
- **Setup**: Display the opening slide on screen ([meet_and_greet.html](file:///Users/rami/Work/FLL/slides/meet_and_greet.html)). Have some loose LEGO bricks on tables for casual building as people arrive.

### Segment 2: Slide Presentation & Official Videos (30 Minutes)
- Launch the interactive slide deck: `slides/meet_and_greet.html`.
- **Key Presentation Topics**:
  - What is FIRST LEGO League Challenge?
  - **Video Clip**: Play the FIRST LEGO League kickoff video spotlight (Slide 4).
  - Theme Reveal: **BIOGLOW™** (Exploring nature, technology, and biodiversity solutions).
  - The 4 Pillars: Core Values, Innovation Project, Robot Design, Robot Game.
  - The Golden Rule of Coaching: **"The Kids Do The Work!"**

### Segment 3: Interactive Icebreaker Challenges (30 Minutes)
Divide students and parents into small mixed teams for two fast-paced, high-fun activities:

1. **Activity A: "Tallest Tower, 10 Bricks, 3 Minutes"**
   - *Goal*: Build the tallest freestanding tower using only 10 random LEGO bricks.
   - *Debrief*: Highlight **Innovation** and **Discovery**—how different teams experimented with base stability.
2. **Activity B: "Bio-Tech Creature & Blindfold Relay"**
   - *Goal*: Build a glowing sea creature or beetle that could exist in a BIOGLOW ecosystem, with one partner giving verbal directions to a blindfolded partner.
   - *Debrief*: Highlight **Teamwork** and **Communication**.

### Segment 4: Snack & Bio-Tech Brain Break (15 Minutes)
- Provide healthy snacks (fruit, granola bars, juice boxes).
- Quick trivia question: *"What is bioluminescence, and how do glowing creatures inspired by nature help scientists?"*

### Segment 5: SPIKE Prime Hands-On Mini Lab & Video Tutorial (30 Minutes)
- Show **Slide 10** with the embedded SPIKE Prime robotics tutorial video.
- Group students (2-3 per kit).
- Hand out the SPIKE Prime Core Hub and battery.
- **Challenge**:
  - Connect the Hub to the SPIKE App.
  - Program a single line of code to make the Hub display a glowing smiley face LED matrix and sound a cheerful chime!
  - Plug in a Medium Motor and program it to spin when the Force Sensor is pressed.

### Segment 6: Season Roadmap, Online Video Hubs & Parent Alignment (20 Minutes)
- **Online Resource Share** (Slide 14): Share [PrimeLessons.org](https://primelessons.org) and the [Official FIRST YouTube Channel](https://www.youtube.com/@FIRSTLEGOLeagueofficial) for learning at home.
- **Meeting Cadence**: Once a week for 2.5 hours. Stress punctuality and consistent attendance.
- **Parent Support Roles**:
  - Snack Coordinator
  - Tournament Transport & Logistics Volunteer
  - Innovation Project Subject Matter Expert connector
  - Practice Judging Volunteer
- **Communication Channel**: Set up team chat (e.g., WhatsApp group, Slack, or email list).

### Segment 7: Team Agreement & Wrap-Up (10 Minutes)
- **Team Code of Conduct**: Have all students and parents sign the "Core Values Agreement".
- **5-Minute Kit Clean-up**: Practice sorting all SPIKE parts into their assigned tray compartments.
- **Closing High-Five**: End with a team cheer!
