What is FIRST® LEGO® League Challenge?

A global STEM program empowering young innovators (Ages 9–12)

🎯 The Goal
Teams of students research real-world problems, engineer autonomous LEGO robots, and compete in friendly tournaments—all while practicing core human values.
🧠 Real-World Problem Solving: Apply science, tech, and engineering to actual societal challenges.
💻 Hands-On Coding & Robotics: Build modular mechanisms & program SPIKE Prime microcontrollers.
👥 Teamwork & Confidence: Learn presentation, communication, and leadership skills for life.
NOTES: Highlight that FLL is much more than just playing with LEGO bricks—it's a complete engineering and public speaking journey.

Season Theme Reveal

BIOGLOW™: Nature & Ecosystem Innovation

Exploring how nature's design inspires modern technology

🌿 The Season Mission

From bioluminescent ocean creatures to forest networks, nature has spent millions of years perfecting bio-systems. Teams will investigate how technological innovations can protect biodiversity or borrow solutions from living organisms!

🤖 Robot Game Field: Complete 15+ themed missions on the official mat (protecting ecosystems, tracking wildlife, harvesting clean energy).
💡 Innovation Project: Research a specific biodiversity or bio-tech problem, create a new solution, and present it to expert judges.
NOTES: Ask students: "Can anyone name a plant or animal that inspired a human invention?" (e.g. Velcro inspired by burrs, sonar from bats, glowing lights from fireflies).

The 4 Pillars of FLL Competition

Teams are judged equally across all 4 categories (25% weight each)

❤️
Core Values
Discovery, Innovation, Impact, Inclusion, Teamwork, and Fun!
💡
Innovation Project
Identify a problem, create a solution, share with experts & community.
🛠️
Robot Design
Engineering design, attachment mechanics, code modularity & strategy.
🎮
Robot Game
Score maximum points in 2.5-minute autonomous matches!
NOTES: Reassure parents that winning the robot game is only 25% of the score. A team with great Core Values and a strong Innovation Project can win high awards!

Core Values & Coaching Philosophy

Gracious Professionalism® & Coopertition® in action

The Golden Rule

"THE KIDS DO THE WORK!"

Coaches and parents are guides and cheerleaders. If a robot needs troubleshooting or a project script needs writing, the students lead the way.

🤝 Inclusion: We respect each other and embrace our differences.
🎉 Fun: We enjoy and celebrate what we do!
NOTES: Emphasize that mistakes are celebrated as learning opportunities ("Failure is just data!").

The Hardware: LEGO® SPIKE™ Prime

Our powerful robotics toolkit for the season

🧠 The Programmable Hub
  • Built-in 6-axis Gyroscope for precise turning
  • 5x5 LED Light Matrix display
  • 6 I/O ports for smart sensors & motors
  • Bluetooth & rechargeable battery
🔌 Motors & Smart Sensors
  • Angular Motors: High precision drive base & attachments
  • Color Sensor: Detect lines, mat colors & target markers
  • Distance Sensor: Ultrasonic wall detection
  • Force Sensor: Touch & push button triggers
NOTES: Show the physical SPIKE Hub to the room. Demonstrate turning on the LED matrix.

The Innovation Project Journey

How students become real-world scientific researchers

1️⃣
Identify
Find a specific real-world problem related to nature & technology.
2️⃣
Design
Invent a novel solution (a device, app, physical model, or bio-system).
3️⃣
Share
Present the prototype to community experts and gather feedback.
4️⃣
Present
Create a 5-minute creative pitch to perform for tournament judges!
NOTES: Explain that expert outreach (calling or interviewing a real scientist or engineer) is a huge bonus point area for judging.

Meeting Schedule & Expectations

Building momentum through consistent weekly practice

🗓️ Meeting Cadence

Once a Week for 2.5 Hours

Longer sessions allow us to get into a deep "flow state"—giving 45 mins to Coding/Robot Game, 45 mins to Innovation Project, and time for team building!

⭐ Student Expectations

  • Attend weekly sessions consistently & on time
  • Encourage teammates and practice Gracious Professionalism
  • Take care of SPIKE Prime parts and participate in 5-min cleanups
NOTES: Reiterate that missing meetings affects the whole team, especially during attachment integration and project rehearsals.
